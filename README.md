# Multiplayer Everything: Flash games

The game files the [Multiplayer Everything Flash Launcher](https://github.com/multiplayereverything/flash-games-launcher)
downloads. When a game file is missing, the launcher offers *Download game*,
takes the files listed for that game in [`catalog.json`](catalog.json), checks
each one against its SHA-256, and saves it in its `games\` folder. You don't need
to download anything from here by hand.

Questions, bugs, or looking for people to play with? Join the [Multiplayer Everything Discord](https://discord.com/invite/P2MSSUf5GX).

## What the mods add

- **Gun Mayhem 2 Online:** the game's local multiplayer, over the internet. The
  host is Player 1; friends join as Players 2, 3 and 4. In the game press F9.
- **Stealth Hunter 2 Online:** the campaign in co-op for up to four players. The
  host runs the world and each player moves their own character on their own PC;
  if anyone dies, the team restarts the mission. In the game press F9, or ONLINE
  CO-OP on the title screen.
- **Bad Ice-Cream Online:** two players on two PCs in Ruffle Together: both PCs run the
  same game and only key presses travel. The host is Player 1 and picks the levels.
  Host with `--coop-host`, join with `--coop-join <ip>:<port>`, or use a room code on
  the central relay. The same file plays co-op on one keyboard in plain Ruffle too.
- **Territory War Online:** red against blue on two to four PCs in Ruffle Together
  (`--coop-keys territory-war`). Players 1 and 3 are red, 2 and 4 blue (against the CPU everyone is red); on a
  turn only that player's keys and clicks count. Turn rotation (default) passes each team's turns round all its
  players; with `--coop-option turns=stick` each player has their own sticks. The host sets up the match with the mouse;
  everyone plays their own turns with arrows, Space, 1-4, X and V.
- **Bow Man Online:** two archers on two PCs, taking turns, in Ruffle Together
  (`--coop-keys bow-man`): VS Human, player 1 W/S to aim and Space to draw and shoot, player 2
  I/K and Enter (on your own PC: W/S or the arrows, Space/Enter/F). Each player can also aim
  with their own mouse. The host's mouse clicks the menus.
- **Ultimate Crab Battle Online:** two to four ships against the same crab, sharing one HP
  bar, in Ruffle Together (`--coop-keys ultimate-crab-battle`). Everyone in the session gets a
  ship and plays with the game's own keys on their own PC: arrows move, A fires, Ctrl skips,
  1-3 pick the difficulty, Y continues. A friend who leaves loses their ship; the rest play on.
- **Air Battle Online:** one to four balloon boats fly the levels together, online
  through Ruffle Together 1.0.3.4 or later (`--coop-keys air-battle`). PLAYERS 1-4 on the
  main menu sets the count. P1 arrows, Z fires, Down/X turn the cannon (or the mouse aims);
  P2 W A D, T, S/Y; P3 and P4 on the numpad. Extra boats wait at the start until their
  player presses a key; the level fails only when every boat is down.
- **Raft Wars Online:** one to four players on the brothers' raft, taking turns at
  the bazooka, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.4+ (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`). The room
  sets the player count; on your turn aim and fire with your own mouse, or arrows/WASD to aim
  and set power, Z/T to fire, X/Y to change ammo. Everyone can buy in the shop with their own
  mouse; the host's mouse runs the menus. A friend who leaves is skipped.
- **Intrusion Online:** the campaign in co-op for up to four soldiers, online through
  Ruffle Together 1.0.3.9 or later. Everyone who joins the room plays (the game sets its own
  key map, so any `--coop-keys` works); each PC plays its own soldier with the original
  controls: WASD or arrows to move and jump, the mouse to aim, click or Space to shoot,
  1-4 for weapons, P to pause. Each player's screen follows their own soldier at normal size
  (when everyone is close it is the same shared view); the team shares the lives, and a soldier
  who ends up too far from the team for a second (flung, left behind) is brought back next to them.
- **Stealth Assassin Online:** up to four assassins sneak through the missions
  together, each on their own PC; every extra player adds one more green target. A
  player who dies watches the rest of the mission; it fails only when everyone is
  down. ONLINE CO-OP on the menu, or F9; MODE switches between room codes (default)
  and Direct IP.
- **Ultimate Assassin Online:** the first Ultimate Assassin for up to four assassins,
  each on their own PC, one more target per extra player; the level fails only when
  everyone is caught. ONLINE CO-OP on the menu, or F9; MODE switches between room
  codes (default) and Direct IP.
- **Ultimate Assassin 2 Online** and **Ultimate Assassin 3 Online:** the same co-op
  for up to four assassins: every guard sees and shoots the nearest player, one more
  target per extra player, the level fails only when everyone is dead. ONLINE CO-OP
  on the menu, or F9; MODE switches between room codes (default) and Direct IP.
- **Smileys War Online:** two to four humans take the bots' places in the game's own
  Deathmatch, Rambo and Team modes, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.6 or later
  (`--coop-keys smileys-war`). HUMANS on the Arena screen starts at the number of players
  who joined; bots fill the rest. Arrows or WASD move and jump, S/Down switches weapon,
  everyone aims and fires with their own mouse (or Space/F to fire and Q/E to aim). Each
  PC has its own camera and its own HUD. If a friend leaves, a bot takes over their
  smiley. With HUMANS 1 it is the original game.
- **Fireboy & Watergirl Online:** the game's own two-player co-op on two PCs through
  Ruffle Together 1.0.3.6 (`--coop-keys fireboy-watergirl`). The host is Fireboy and the
  friend is Watergirl; each presses arrows or W/A/D on their own PC, and P pauses. The
  host's mouse runs the menus. Only the ad, the online hiscore board and the sponsor links
  are taken out.

- **Dad 'n Me Online:** the single-player beat 'em up with players 2 to 4 added, online
  through Ruffle Together 1.0.3.7 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`). Every
  player uses the game's own keys on their own PC (arrows to move, A to punch, S for the
  second punch) and has their own HUD with their name at the top left; the host clicks the
  menus. Friendly fire is off unless the host turns it on (`--coop-option friendlyfire=1`). Enemies go
  for the nearest player, one shared
  screen follows the group, and a knocked-out player gets up again after 6 seconds while
  anyone is still standing. A friend who leaves is removed and the rest play on. With
  one player it is the original game.

- **Chaos Faction 2 Online:** the platform fighter's Deathmatch for 2 to 6 players through Ruffle
  Together 1.0.5 (key map `chaos-faction-2`, built in). Players 1 and 2 are the game's own Keyboard 1
  and Keyboard 2; players 3 to 6 take the game's other four fighters, which are CPUs in the original
  (their cards still say CPU on the players page; in the match each one is its player's, with a name
  tag over it). Every player uses the game's own keys on their own PC: arrows to move, jump and
  shield, Z and X to attack, D to drop a weapon, Shift to punch while holding one, T to taunt; the
  host's Space pauses and the host's mouse runs the menus, and each friend clicks only their own card.
  Fighters left over with fewer players stay CPUs if the host keeps them on. The campaign is for
  everyone too: each friend gets their own fighter next to player 1 in all 15 levels (with their card's
  look and their own keys), and a level is lost only when every player is out of lives. Survival stays
  single-player (the host plays, the friends watch). With one player it is the original game.

- **Zombotron Online:** the physics shooter-platformer with players 2 to 4 added, online
  through Ruffle Together 1.0.3.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`; the game
  then sets its own keys). Every player walks with A/D or arrows, jumps with W, Up or Space, uses
  doors, chests and shops with S, Down, E or F, reloads with R, switches weapon with Q, heals with
  H, and aims and shoots with their own mouse. Each PC follows its own hero with its own camera
  (or one shared team camera, a host setting, with a zoom setting); names float over the heroes
  and a panel per player shows health, armor, weapon, ammo, coins and aidkits. Enemies go for
  any hero, everyone picks things up for themselves, missions count for the team and whoever
  reaches the exit takes the team on. A downed player gets up next to a friend with jump. Shops
  use the coins and the mouse of whoever opened them. Friendly fire and players bumping into
  each other are host settings, off by default. A friend who leaves is removed and the rest play
  on. With one player it is the original game.

- **Electricman 2 HS Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.7
  (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`). A team fights the whole tournament in the game's one arena, all
  on the game's own keys on their own PC, each in their own colour with their own health, battery and name. Enemies
  go for their nearest player who is up, and there is one extra enemy per extra player. A player out of health stays
  down until the match ends; the match is lost only when everyone is down. A friend who leaves is removed and the
  rest play on. With one player it is the original game.

- **Ultimate Flash Sonic Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`,
  `--coop-players 2-4`). Everyone moves with arrows or WASD and jumps with Space or Z on their own PC; the host clicks
  the menus. Player 1 plays the character picked in the menu and players 2-4 get Sonic, Tails or Knuckles, each with a
  name tag. One shared screen follows the team and keeps everyone in it; a player held back at the edge for 3 seconds
  jumps to the one in front. Rings and lives are the team's: a player who dies comes back next to a friend after 3
  seconds, and the act restarts only when nobody is left. Passing the finish sign takes the whole team out. A friend
  who leaves is removed and the rest play on. With one player it is the original game.

- **The Skullkid Online:** a second player added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`,
  `--coop-players 2`). Player 2 is a second, blue kid with a name tag; everyone uses arrows and Space on their own PC and
  the host clicks the menus. On the chainsaw floor each kid walks and saws on their own, and whoever reaches the edge
  of the screen scrolls it; on the shootout and boss floors the kids walk as a team and each ducks, shoots or saws on
  their own, with one shared health bar. A friend who leaves is removed and the other plays on. With one player it is
  the original game.

- **Run 2 Online:** 2 to 4 players online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`,
  `--coop-players 2-4`). Run 2 turns its whole tunnel around its one runner, so instead of sharing one tunnel every
  player runs their own copy of the game in the same session: their own Runner or Skater, level and camera, on the
  game's own keys on their own PC (arrows or A/D, Space/Up/W to jump, R to restart). Friends pick Runner with Left
  and Skater with Right; the level select works by keys. Friends start from the host's unlocked levels. Only the
  host's game plays music, and only the host can pause and use the mouse (editor, options). A friend who leaves is
  removed and the rest play on. With one player it is the original game.

- **Madness: Project Nexus Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.6
  (`--coop-keys stealth-hunter-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`), in Story and Arena. Player 1 is the mission's hero and each
  friend plays a character of their own with the same stats and gear. Everyone has their own camera, HUD, aim and
  mouse, on the game's own keys (WASD or arrows, Q swap, Space dodge and use, R reload, E pick up, Shift bullet time).
  Friends can click the menus too. Any player can use a door or a panel, and a door takes the team along. A player who
  dies gets up after 8 seconds while anyone else is up; everyone down is the game's own rule. A friend who leaves
  becomes an AI teammate and the rest play on. With one player it is the original game.

- **The World's Hardest Game 2 Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8
  (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`). Everyone uses arrows or WASD on their own PC and is a square in
  one of the game's own colours with their name above it; each player's deaths are in the top bar. Every player has
  their own checkpoint and dies on their own. Coins are shared, and a coin goes back when the player who took it dies
  (by the game's rule for that coin). Any player can take a key, and its door stays open for the level. A level ends
  when all coins are taken and everyone stands in the goal together. The host clicks the menus and CONTINUE. A friend
  who leaves is removed and the rest play on. With one player it is the original game.

- **The World's Hardest Game Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.6
  (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`). Everyone uses arrows or WASD on their own PC and is a
  square of their own colour with their name above it; each player's deaths are in the top bar. Each player
  has their own checkpoint. Coins are shared, and the coins you took go back when you die. A level ends when
  all coins are taken and everyone stands in the goal together. A friend who leaves is removed and the rest
  play on. With one player it is the original game.

- **Hobo Online:** the street brawler with players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together
  1.0.3.9 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`, `--coop-players 2-4`; the game then sets its own keys).
  Every player uses the game's own keys on their own PC: arrows to walk, A to punch, S to kick, the
  game's combos, and picks up and throws cans and bottles for themselves. Players 2-4 are tinted
  blue, green and orange, with their own health portrait at the top left and their name over their
  head. One shared screen: walking scrolls the street for everyone, and it won't scroll a friend off
  the screen. Enemies go for the nearest player who is up. A player whose health runs out lies down
  and gets up 8 seconds later with half health while anyone is still standing; when everyone is
  down it is the game's own game over. A friend who leaves is removed and the rest play on. The
  host clicks the menus; checkpoint passwords work as in the game. With one player it is the
  original game.

- **Flash Chess Online:** two players on two PCs through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys bow-man`,

- **Raze Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`,

  host runs out of lives. Only the host uses the menus, pauses and views scores. A friend who leaves takes their soldier

- **Portal: The Flash Version Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`,

  energy balls, electric fields, elevators and the level triggers work for every player; each player carries their own
  box, and grabbing one a friend holds takes it. One death restarts the chamber for everyone, and the next chamber starts
  when everyone is through the exit door. Only the host uses the menus. A friend who leaves is removed and the rest play
  on. With one player it is the original game.

- **Strike Force Heroes Online:** players 2 to 4 added, online through Ruffle Together 1.0.8 (`--coop-keys gun-mayhem-2`,
  `--coop-players 2-4`; the game sets its own keys when it starts). Every friend gets a soldier of their own next to the

## Test status

**Stealth Hunter 2 co-op for Ruffle Together, build of 2026-10-31** (`stealthhunter2_online.swf`,
`stealthhunter2_online_direct.swf`, the same file). The game has no local multiplayer of its own, so
this build adds it: 2 to 4 players, each on their own PC with the normal keys, through Ruffle
Together 1.0.3.7 (`--coop-keys stealth-hunter-2`). Every player has their own camera that follows
their own player; the HUD stays on screen. Tested on one PC with the 1.0.3.7 release: replays

sessions with 2, 3 and 4 players through a relay and directly with no desync, and the in-game co-op
lab (17 of 17). Not yet played by two real players on two PCs. Players more than three screens apart
see black at the far edge of their view. Report problems in the
[issues](https://github.com/multiplayereverything/flash-games/issues).

**Smileys War for Ruffle Together, build of 2026-11-06** (`SmileysWar_online.swf`): 2 to 4 humans
through Ruffle Together 1.0.6, each PC with its own camera and HUD. Tested on one PC: replays identical
frame for frame (and identical to the original game with one human), Ruffle Together sessions with
2, 3 and 4 players through a relay and directly with no desync, a friend leaving mid-match, and two
real windows. Not yet played by two real players on two PCs.

**Dad 'n Me for Ruffle Together, build of 2026-10-30** (`DadNMe_online.swf`): 1 to 4 players through
Ruffle Together 1.0.3.7. Tested on one PC: replays identical frame for frame (and identical to the original
game with one player), Ruffle Together sessions with 2, 3 and 4 players through a relay and directly with no
desync, a 4-player run by test bots through every boss to the ending with no desync, and the same run with
one friend leaving halfway (the other three played on in sync). Not yet played by
real players on two PCs.

**Chaos Faction 2 for Ruffle Together, build of 2026-11-01** (`ChaosFaction2_online.swf`, 1.2: campaign with

## Layout

```
catalog.json              every file: name, game id, path, size, sha256, date
info.json                 each game's mod version and what changed (edit by hand; the website reads it)
                          and its launcher settings ("settings": checkboxes/dropdowns, copied into catalog.json)
games/<game id>/*.swf     the files themselves
tools/update-catalog.ps1  rebuilds catalog.json from games/
tools/file-notes.json     one-line description per file name
```

`game` in the catalog is the launcher's game id, and `name` is the file name the
launcher expects in its `games\` folder.

## Adding or updating a file

1. Put the `.swf` in `games/<game id>/` with the name the launcher looks for
   (see `launcher/games/README.txt` in the launcher repository).
2. Add a line for a new file to `tools/file-notes.json`.
3. Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\update-catalog.ps1`.
4. Commit the file and `catalog.json` together. Launchers see the new SHA-256 and
   re-download a file only when someone asks for it.

Each file must stay under GitHub's 100 MB limit; the current ones are 2 to 9 MB.
